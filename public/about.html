About the game

Northward

Northward is an open-world co-op adventure about sailing a scrappy ship through a northern archipelago, uncovering the mystery of a fallen star, and exploring the remnants of an ancient civilization.

Explore a variety of islands and biomes, bustling harbors and deep waters. Survive the harsh climate and recruit new crew-members to help you on your journey. Upgrade your vessel, gather useful resources and equipment, and follow the clues that lead farther north.

Made by Yore & Yonder, Northward is a mix of mystery, cooperative exploration, and early-2000s animated-adventure spirit. With inspirations ranging from Outer Wilds and Sea of Thieves to Treasure Planet and Atlantis: The Lost Empire.
